Recovery Protocol — Operator Guide

Getting Started

1
Open the platform on your phone or laptop. You'll see the login screen.
2
Select your team from the dropdown, enter your team access code (given by the organiser), and choose a callsign (your operator name).
3
Each team has 3 operator slots. All 3 operators share the same wallet, inventory, and progress.
Your starting resources

150 CIT$ — the only currency in the recovery system.

0 Core Energy (NRG) — this is your real score. The team with the most energy wins.

Rules

⚠ STRICTLY PROHIBITED

The use of AI is strictly forbidden.

This includes but is not limited to: ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, Gemini, or any other AI assistant, chatbot, or code generation tool.

Any team caught using AI will be immediately disqualified.

This event tests YOUR skills — problem-solving, teamwork, and creativity. Not a machine's.

General Rules
  • Each team has exactly 3 operators. No substitutions during the event.
  • Do not share flags, codes, or solutions with other teams.
  • Do not attack the platform, other teams, or any infrastructure.
  • Respect the admins and mission validators — their decision is final.
  • Have fun.

Phase I — Challenges Digital Arena

Three types of challenges are available:

Competitive Programming (CP)

  • Solve problems on HackerRank (link on each challenge card).
  • When all hidden tests pass, HackerRank gives you a completion key.
  • Come back to CIT:404 and submit the key in the CIT{...} format.

Capture the Flag (CTF)

  • Crypto, OSINT, Misc, Steganography, and Web challenges.
  • Some have downloadable files or external links — look for the button on the card.
  • Find the flag and submit it in CIT{...} format.

Data Core Retrieval (DCR)

  • 15 SQL investigation missions across 4 difficulty levels.
  • You get access to a read-only database with a built-in SQL console.
  • Read the briefing, query the data, and type your answer.
  • Some missions are locked until you solve their prerequisite.
Hints

Stuck? You can buy Hint Level 1 (30 CIT$) or Hint Level 2 (75 CIT$) from the Market, then use them on a challenge. The hint text appears on the card. Not every challenge has hints.

First Blood

The first team to solve a challenge gets a bonus reward — extra CIT$ and energy on top of the normal payout.

> CIT$ is not just a score. > CIT$ is your power during the next phase.

Phase II — Missions Field Operations

You will be sent to real locations across Rabat to complete field missions.

1
Read the location hint on the mission card. Figure out where to go.
2
When you arrive, find an admin on site. They will give you the access code.
3
Enter the access code on the platform. The real location name appears once you unlock.
4
Choose a difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard). Higher difficulty = higher cost, higher reward, less time.
5
The platform assigns you a random task. Complete it and show the proof to the admin.
6
The admin validates or fails your mission. Payout is automatic.
Supply Run

Out of CIT$? The Supply Run is a free, repeatable mission. Complete it to earn 200 CIT$ — enough to get back in the game.

Mission items (from the Market)
  • Insurance (100 CIT$) — if you fail, recover 50% of the entry cost.
  • Location Scan (80 CIT$) — reveals GPS coordinates for a mission.
  • Mission Resign (50 CIT$) — abandon an active mission to free the slot.
  • Mission Reroll (40 CIT$) — redo a completed/failed mission.
  • Time Boost (60 CIT$) — +10 minutes on your timer.
  • Double Reward (120 CIT$) — doubles CIT$ and energy on completion.

Phase III — Endgame Recovery Protocol

Throughout the event, you will collect physical fragments scattered around INPT.

When the Endgame phase opens, assemble all the fragments into a single flag and submit it on the platform.

> One flag. One input. One chance to finish the recovery.

The Story

As the event progresses, story fragments unlock in the Story tab. Each phase reveals a new piece of the narrative. Pay attention — the story may not be what it seems.

Scoring

What matters

Core Energy (NRG) is your score. The team with the most energy at the end wins.

CIT$ is your currency — spend it on missions, items, and hints. It is NOT the score.

How you earn
  • Solve challenges → CIT$ + NRG
  • First blood bonus → extra CIT$ + NRG
  • Complete missions → CIT$ + NRG
  • DCR missions → CIT$ + NRG
  • Submit the final flag → CIT$ + NRG

Quick Tips

  • All 3 operators share one wallet — coordinate your spending.
  • Flags are always in CIT{...} format (case-insensitive).
  • The /scoreboard page shows live standings on the venue screen.
  • Keep your browser tab open — you'll get live notifications from THE CORE.
  • If you run out of money, do the Supply Run.
